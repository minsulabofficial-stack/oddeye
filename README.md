# Odd Eye

**See the structure behind your AI-generated app before asking AI to fix it.**

Odd Eye helps vibe coders understand the flow, conditions, and connections inside an app without reading code syntax.

It is built for people creating apps with tools like **Cursor, Lovable, Bolt, Replit, v0, and ChatGPT** who can build with AI, but struggle when the app becomes hard to understand or fix.

---

## Why Odd Eye exists

AI can help you build an app quickly.

But when something breaks, the hard part is often not writing more code. The hard part is knowing **what part of the app should be changed**.

Many vibe coders run into the same problem:

- AI changes the wrong part of the app.
- A small fix breaks an existing feature.
- The app works, but the internal flow is unclear.
- The user knows what they want, but cannot explain the exact logic to AI.
- Repeated fix requests waste time, tokens, and confidence.

Odd Eye is designed to solve that gap.

---

## What Odd Eye does

Odd Eye gives non-developers a developer-like view of their app.

Instead of showing raw code, it helps users see:

- what the app does
- how each function connects
- what conditions affect the flow
- where data or decisions move
- which part should be fixed
- what to tell an AI coding tool next

The goal is simple:

> Understand the app before asking AI to change it.

---

## Core workflow

1. **Describe the program you want to build**  
   Explain your idea, feature, or app as clearly as possible.

2. **Answer clarification questions**  
   Odd Eye checks the intent and context before generating the structure.

3. **Review the graph**  
   See the app as connected logic instead of isolated screens or features.

4. **Select the part that needs correction**  
   Identify the function or logic area that does not match your intent.

5. **Send the completed structure to your AI coding tool**  
   Use the graph and explanation to guide Cursor, Lovable, Bolt, or another AI coding service.
